##This folder contains the code and output files from simulations of a model
##of a fungal plant pathogen targetted with (a) fungicide, (b) a haploid-acting 
##gene drive and/or (c) a diploid-acting gene drive.

##It is described in full in Garner, W., Alkemade, J. A. and Barraclough, T.G. 2026
##Evaluating the potential to control fungal plant pathogens using gene drive technology

##There are five flavours of model contained here

##a) No control applied to the fungal population
## Garner_NoControl.R and NoControlResults.csv

##b) Fungicide applied to the fungal population, either single, alternating by year or within-years
## Garner_FungicideModels.R and FungicideModelResults.csv

##c) A gene drive that has a negative fitness effect on a haploid stage of the life-cycle
## Garner_HaploidGeneDrive.R and HaploidGeneDriveResults.csv

##d) A gene drive that has a negative effect on the diploid stage of the life-cycle
## Garner_DiploidGeneDrive.R and DiploidGeneDriveResults.csv

##e) A combination of fungicide and diploid-acting gene drive
## Garner_DiploidGeneDriveFungicide.R and DiploidGeneDriveFungicideResults.csv
